About Us

Urban and Architect Solution is a modern architectural and urban planning firm based in Bogura, Bangladesh. Founded with a vision to deliver high-quality, BNBC-compliant designs, we specialize in residential, commercial, and master planning projects.

Our team focuses on functional, aesthetic, and sustainable architecture. From concept design and 3D visualization to detailed AutoCAD drawings and approval documentation, we handle the complete design process.

Our Philosophy

We believe in designing spaces that are not only beautiful but also practical, cost-effective, and future-ready.
